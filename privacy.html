Pennee privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Pennee is a money app for Android. This page says what data it uses, where that data goes, and how you can remove it.

Your money data stays on your phone

Your payments, amounts, shop names, notes, accounts, budgets, goals and bank statements are stored only on your phone. Pennee never sends them to us or to anyone else.

  • Payment notifications. If you allow notification access, Pennee reads notifications from the payment apps you pick, to record payments. It reads them on your phone and keeps the result on your phone. It ignores notifications from other apps.
  • Statements. Statement files you import are read on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • Backups. If Android backup is on for your phone, Android can copy Pennee's database to your own Google account, like it does for other apps. Google handles this backup. We cannot see it. You can switch it off in your phone's settings.
  • Backup files. When you export a backup file, it goes only where you choose to save or share it.

What is sent while Pennee is in testing

Pennee is in testing now. To find problems and make the app better, it sends usage data while you use it:

  • which screens you open and how long each one stays open,
  • which buttons you tap, by a fixed button name (for example "settings-button"), never by what the button shows,
  • when the app opens, closes and comes back,
  • app errors,
  • the app version, the Android version and the phone model,
  • a random ID that Pennee makes on your phone. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account.

Usage data never has amounts, shop names, notes or any of your records in it.

Feedback

When you send feedback in the app, we get the text you write, the last screens you opened, the app version, the phone model and the random ID. Do not put private details in your message. Feedback is sent to the developer's Telegram so they can read it fast.

Where data is kept

Usage data and feedback are kept in a Supabase database in Singapore. Only the developer can read them. We keep them until testing ends, then we delete them. We do not sell data, we do not show ads, and we do not share data with anyone else.

App updates

Pennee checks GitHub Pages for new versions of its screens and downloads them. This check sends no data of yours.

Delete your data

  • To delete your money data, use Settings › Delete all data in the app, or uninstall Pennee.
  • To delete your usage data and feedback, send feedback in the app that says "delete my data". We delete everything linked to your phone's random ID.

Children

Pennee is not made for children under 13.

Contact

Send feedback in the app (the message button at the top of the main screens). We answer questions about privacy there.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
